The Genetic Laboratory

The Eridu Genesis explicitly describes gods mixing "clay of the earth" with "divine essence" to create humans. This is the earliest known written account of genetic manipulation.

The Seven Cities of Creation

The text names seven cities where humans were created: Eridu, Bad-tibira, Larak, Sippara, Shuruppak, Ubursag, and Nippur. These may represent different genetic laboratories or breeding centers.

"They fashioned man of clay, mixed his flesh with the blood of the gods, and gave him life." — Eridu Genesis Tablet

The Workforce Purpose

Humans were created specifically to work in the gods' gardens and mines. The "toil and suffering" described matches slave labor conditions in advanced technological facilities.
