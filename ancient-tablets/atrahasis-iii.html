Sumerian

Atrahasis Tablet III

The Gods' Departure • c. 1700 BCE

The Final Warning

Tablet III describes the Anunnaki preparing to leave Earth after the flood. Enki provided Atrahasis with "secrets of the gods" before their departure - advanced knowledge to preserve for future generations.

The Hidden Knowledge

The gods entrusted humanity with astronomy, agriculture, and metallurgy before departing. This knowledge was too advanced for the era and suggests deliberate seeding of civilization.

"Take these tablets, preserve them through generations, for the gods shall return and question what was learned in their absence." — Atrahasis Tablet III

The Prophecy of Return

The tablet ends with prophecy that the gods would return "when the celestial markers align again" - suggesting cyclical rather than linear interaction between worlds.
