c. 3400 BCE

Pre-Cuneiform Tokens

The First Data Storage System

The Clay Revolution

Before writing, Sumerians used clay tokens to track goods. These cone, sphere, and disk shapes represented quantities of grain, livestock, and textiles.

The Accounting Innovation

Each token type had specific meaning. Over 300 distinct shapes have been found, suggesting sophisticated data encoding on clay - the precursor to digital storage.

"The tokens were sealed inside clay balls to prevent tampering - the world's first secure database." — Archaeological Evidence from Uruk
