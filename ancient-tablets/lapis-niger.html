Sumerian

The Lapis Niger

Oldest Known Sumerian Text • c. 2500 BCE

The Black Stone

The Lapis Niger (Black Stone) was an early Sumerian text written on a black basalt slab. It predates other known writings and contains references to "rulers who came from the sky."

The Original Kings

The text lists kings who ruled for tens of thousands of years - impossible lifespans for humans but reasonable for those with advanced life extension technology.

"Before the flood, the kings of the sky dwelt among us, teaching the ways of civilization." — Lapis Niger Fragment

The Missing Original

The original stone was lost during wartime. Copies may have been deliberately altered to obscure the advanced technology origins of early civilization - evidence of historical manipulation.
