Constellation Tablets

Star Maps

The MUL.APIN

The "Star Book" lists 71 constellations including those unknown to modern astronomy. The "Scorpion of the Euphrates" and "Great Twins" show different celestial patterns.

The Ziqpu Stars

Tablets describe "zepped" - celestial ropes connecting stars. The "Turn of the Scorpion" marks the equinox - sophisticated astronomy etched in clay.

"When the scorpion rises, the year is completed and the day expands." — MUL.APIN Tablet
