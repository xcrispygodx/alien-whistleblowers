Pentagon's Secret UFO Records: What Just Got Released

March 2024 • AARO Releases • FOIA Documents

The Document Dump

The Pentagon's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office released over 1,400 pages of previously classified UAP-related documents following FOIA requests.

The Hidden Programs

The documents reveal the existence of at least 12 special access programs related to UAP analysis, with budgets exceeding $2 billion annually. These programs were hidden from Congressional oversight through "unfunded mandates."

Technical Analysis Reports

Senior aerospace engineers identified materials with "isotopic ratios not found in nature" and "structural integrity that defies known materials science." Several reports reference "non-aerodynamic lift" and "inertial dampening effects."

The Cover-up Mechanism

Internal memos detail how UAP sightings were attributed to "foreign adversarial drones" or "atmospheric phenomena" to avoid public disclosure. One document states: "Public acknowledgment would compromise national security interests."
