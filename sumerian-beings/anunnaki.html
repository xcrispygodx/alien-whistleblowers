The Royal Race

The Anunnaki ("those who from heaven came") were worshipped as deities in ancient Sumer, but the original texts describe them as physical beings from a distant planet.

The Descent to Earth

Sumerian tablets describe the Anunnaki journeying from their home star (Nibiru) to Earth to mine gold. They needed workers, creating humans through genetic manipulation of existing hominids.

"The Anunnaki were mighty; they labored not, they knew no fatigue. Their distress was great." — Enuma Elish, c. 1800 BCE

The Igigi Revolt

The Igigi (lesser gods) rebelled against mining labor, prompting Enki and Ninhursag to create humanity as slave labor. This narrative matches genetic engineering descriptions found in multiple tablets.
