The Advanced Warning

Enki warned Ziusudra through multiple messengers and signs about the coming flood. This suggests an organized communication and evacuation protocol, not supernatural prophecy.

The Ark Construction

The vessel was built according to precise specifications with "five decks and nine compartments." Advanced engineering knowledge was required - possibly provided by the gods themselves.

"Build a ship, make it watertight, build it with five decks, within it I will save you and your seed." — Eridu Genesis Deluge Account

The Preservation Protocol

The survival of seeds, animals, and humans suggests a planned preservation program - not divine wrath but strategic evacuation before planetary catastrophe or warfare.
