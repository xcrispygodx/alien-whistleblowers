The Perfect Human

Adapa was created by Ea (Enki) as a model human with perfect wisdom. The texts describe him as lacking only "immortality and the ability to see the gods" - suggesting he was a prototype.

The Wisdom Granted

Adapa received advanced knowledge: meteorology (controlling weather), medicine (healing without sickness), and divine secrets. When he healed the sick, "life was in his hand" - possibly referring to advanced medical technology.

"Ea had created him, had made him like one of the gods, perfect in wisdom and understanding." — Adapa Myth

The Forbidden Knowledge

When offered food and drink by the gods that would grant immortality, Adapa refused, choosing mortality to avoid divine scrutiny. This suggests the gods feared humans with too much knowledge of their true nature.
