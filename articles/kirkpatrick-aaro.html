The Scientific Approach

Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick, former AARO director, provided unprecedented detail about the office's methodology in analyzing UAP data. His approach emphasized scientific rigor over speculation.

The Data Analysis

"We analyzed over 300 cases involving multiple sensor types," Kirkpatrick explained. "Approximately 10% remain truly unidentified — not due to lack of data, but because they exhibit physics we cannot currently model."

"The acceleration profiles we observe would liquefy any biological occupant and tear apart conventional aircraft structures." — Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick, AARO Director

The Energy Signatures

His team detected electromagnetic signatures from several UAP events that "do not match any known propulsion or power generation system." Spectral analysis revealed materials interactions that "should not be possible."

The International Component

AARO confirmed cooperation with "Five Eyes" partners and NATO allies on UAP investigations. Foreign militaries have provided "substantially similar" data and observations.
