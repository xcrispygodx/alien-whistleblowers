David Grusch's Historic Congressional Testimony

June 2023 • House Oversight Committee • Classification Level: TS//SCI//NOFORN
FBI UAP Photo - Public Domain
FBI UAP Photo - Public Domain

On June 5, 2023, David Charles Grusch, a former member of the U.S. Air Force and National Reconnaissance Office senior intelligence officer, provided sworn testimony to Congress about Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) and alleged reverse engineering programs.

The Opening Statement

Grusch began by stating he was testifying "about my personal observations and analysis of the UAP issue, based on my operational experience and additional work I performed outside of my official capacity."

Key Revelations Under Oath

Under questioning, Grusch made several unprecedented claims:

  • "We have intact craft and biological materials" recovered from UAP
  • These programs operate without congressional oversight through "misappropriated" funds
  • He identified specific contractors involved in "legacy programs" dating back decades
  • Confirmed the existence of a "UAP special access program" outside normal channels
"I have reason to believe that many of these craft are made from materials not of this world... They have been studied for decades in special access programs." — David Grusch, Under Oath Testimony

The Whistleblower Protection Angle

Grusch's testimony was protected under the Intelligence Community Whistleblower Protection Act. He stated he faced retaliation after attempting to bring these issues to light through proper channels.

His claims have sparked new investigations and renewed calls for greater transparency from the Pentagon about UAP programs that have operated in the shadows since the 1940s.
