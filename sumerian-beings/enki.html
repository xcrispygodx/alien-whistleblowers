The Water Bearer's Wisdom

Enki (Ea in Akkadian) was the god of water, wisdom, and crafts. Tablets describe him as master of advanced knowledge in metallurgy, agriculture, and "the secrets of life itself."

The Genetic Laboratory

The Eridu Genesis describes Enki's temple as a facility where "the seed of life" was manipulated. He created humans using "clay of the abyss" and "divine blood" according to multiple cuneiform sources.

"I have fused divine essence with earthly clay to create workers for the gods' labor." — Eridu Genesis Tablet

The Great Flood Warning

Enki defied his brother Enlil to warn humanity of the Great Deluge. His actions saved Ziusudra (Noah) and his family, preserving human lineage. This suggests Enki viewed humans as more than mere slaves.
