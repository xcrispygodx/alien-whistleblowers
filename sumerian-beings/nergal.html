The Red Planet Connection

Nergal was associated with the planet Mars and the color red. His domain was the "underworld" but also the "mountain of the east" - perhaps a base of operations.

The Battle Technology

Sumerian texts describe Nergal wielding "the seven winds of destruction" and "fire without flame." These could be descriptions of directed energy weapons or advanced propulsion systems.

"Nergal rides forth with fire and wind, striking down his enemies with weapons of the sky." — Nergal and Ereshkigal Epic

The Military Leadership

Nergal's battles against the forces of chaos may represent conflicts between advanced factions. The "great battle" that Nergal won could be historical wars using superior technology.
