About This Archive

Documenting Government Whistleblower Testimony

The Mission

This archive documents testimony from government officials, military personnel, and defense contractors who have come forward with information about Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) and alleged recovered alien technology programs.

The Evidence

All articles are based on public testimony, declassified documents, congressional hearings, and sworn statements from individuals with legitimate access to classified information. Sources are cited where possible.

Disclaimer

This site presents claims made by witnesses under oath or in official capacities. The veracity of extraordinary claims should be evaluated critically and independently. We provide a platform for information that has been officially testified.
