Sumerian

Utu: The Sun God

Shamash Divine Judge • c. 2100 BCE

The Solar Chariot

Utu (Shamash) traveled across the sky in a "radiant chariot" drawn by "fiery steeds." The Sumerian description of solar travel matches modern interpretations of orbital vehicles.

The Judge of Justice

As god of justice, Utu was said to "see all deeds" from his celestial vantage point. This omniscient surveillance capability could only be achieved from orbit, suggesting satellite technology.

"Shamash rises in the sky, beholds all that is done below, and none can escape his judgment." — Utu Hymn, c. 2000 BCE

The Golden Throne

The "throne of burnished gold" described in Utu's temple may have been a control seat or advanced console. Ancient art depicts the sun god seated before a circular array of lights.
