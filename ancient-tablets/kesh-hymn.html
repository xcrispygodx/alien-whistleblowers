Sumerian

The Kesh Temple Hymn

Advanced Technology Description • c. 2500 BCE

The Perfect Artifact

The hymn describes a temple hoop that was "the work of the gods, perfect in design." It had "no flaw, no defect" and was so precisely balanced it could spin perpetually - possibly a description of gyroscopic or anti-gravity technology.

The Radiant Throne

The temple contained a "throne of burnished gold" with "jewels like stars" that provided "light without fire." This advanced lighting technology was displayed as divine power.

"Its gleam goes up to heaven, it shines like the abode of the gods. Perfect is its workmanship, firm are its supports." — Kesh Temple Hymn

The Food and Drink

The hymn mentions "food and drink" that sustains the temple's divine inhabitants. These provisions may have been advanced nutritional or life-extension supplies, not ordinary sustenance.
