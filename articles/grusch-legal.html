David Grusch: The Whistleblower Protection Case

July 2023 • IC IG Complaint • Legal Proceedings

The Official Complaint

In May 2023, David Grusch filed a formal complaint with the Intelligence Community Inspector General, alleging retaliation for raising concerns about UAP programs through proper channels.

Protected Disclosure

The complaint stated Grusch attempted to communicate with both the CIA and NRO leadership regarding "illegal covert operations involving recovered exotic craft and biological materials." He argues these constitute protected whistleblowing under the Intelligence Community Whistleblower Protection Act.

"I made protected disclosures about these programs and subsequently faced reprisals including removal from assignment and denial of promotion." — David Grusch, IC IG Complaint

Congressional Investigation

The House Oversight Committee launched an investigation following Grusch's testimony. Multiple intelligence committees received briefings about the alleged "legacy programs" that have operated without oversight for decades.

The Security Clearance Revocation

After speaking publicly, Grusch's security clearances were revoked. His legal team argues this violates whistleblower protection statutes designed to shield those who expose wrongdoing from retaliation.
