Who Risked Their Reputation for Truth

The Pioneers of Disclosure

Throughout history, high-profile individuals have risked their careers and reputations to speak publicly about UFO phenomena:

  • General Nathan Twining
    USAF Chief of Staff (1953-1957)

    First official military assessment stating flying saucers were "real and not visionary or fictitious" in the famous Twining Memo.

  • J. Allen Hynek
    Astrophysicist, CIA Consultant, Project Blue Book

    Converted from skeptic to believer after reviewing classified cases. Created the "Close Encounters" classification system.

  • Dr. John Mack
    Harvard Psychiatrist, Pulitzer Prize winner

    Studied alien abduction cases despite professional ridicule. Published "Abduction" risking tenure and credibility.

  • Stanton Friedman
    Nuclear Physicist, Government Researcher

    First civilian to investigate Roswell. Authored over 80 papers on UFO phenomena despite industry blacklisting.

  • Dr. Steven M. Greer
    Emergency Medicine Physician

    Founded CSETI and compiled testimonies from 400+ military witnesses. Built the Disclosure Project risking career.
