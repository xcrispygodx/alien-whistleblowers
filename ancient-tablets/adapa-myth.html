Sumerian

The Adapa Myth

The Perfect Man • c. 1800 BCE

The Warning From Heaven

Adapa was granted wisdom to predict the future by observing bird flight patterns and celestial signs. This advanced astronomical knowledge was attributed to divine gift.

The Food and Water Test

When offered "food of life" and "water of life" by the gods, Adapa refused, believing them to be death sentences. This limited human potential to access advanced longevity technology.

"Ea warned me: what they offer is death, not life. Do not accept their gifts." — Adapa Myth, c. 1800 BCE

The Divine Disappointment

The tablet ends with the gods lamenting that Adapa was "almost perfect" - suggesting deliberate limitation of human capabilities through misinformation and deception.
