The Descent of Inanna

Underworld Journey • c. 2300 BCE

The Seven Gate Protocol

Inanna must pass through seven gates, surrendering garments at each. This "strip-search" protocol matches security procedures for entering restricted facilities, not mythological afterlife beliefs.

The Naked Exposure

"She was stripped of all power" upon reaching the throne - suggesting advanced technology was deactivated through security protocols. The "eye of death" represents surveillance systems.

"At each gate, the watcher demanded a garment. By the seventh, she stood naked before the queen of the underworld." — Descent of Inanna, Tablet I

The Escape Hatch

Inanna's rescue required intervention from Ninshubur and Enki providing the "food and water of life" - emergency medical protocols for a comrade trapped in a dangerous facility.
