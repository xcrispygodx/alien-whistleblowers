The Secretary's Acknowledgment

Ronald Moultrie, Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence, acknowledged that UAP investigations involve "national security concerns" that extend beyond simple aerial phenomena.

The Threat Assessment

"We cannot rule out that some UAP represent capabilities of strategic threat to our forces," Moultrie stated under oath. This marked the first official acknowledgment of potential threat from non-human origin technology.

"The capabilities demonstrated by these objects exceed our current understanding of physics and engineering." — Ronald Moultrie, Under Secretary of Defense

The Investigation Scope

Moultrie confirmed that OSD coordinates with multiple agencies to track UAP, including the CIA, NSA, and NGA. The investigation spans "all domains" - air, space, sea, and potentially cyber.
