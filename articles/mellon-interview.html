Christopher Mellon: The Former Deputy Assistant Secretary Speaks

January 2024 • Interview • Inside Knowledge
UAP Researchers - Public Domain

High-Level Access

Christopher Mellon served as Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence during the Clinton and Bush administrations. His testimony about UAP is based on direct access to classified programs.

The Navy Videos

Mellon confirmed that the 2004 Nimitz and 2015-2016 USS Theodore Roosevelt UAP videos were shared with high-level Pentagon officials. "These weren't dismissed as foreign aircraft. Everyone knew they were something else entirely."

"The technology demonstrated in these videos is beyond anything in our military inventory or any known foreign capability." — Christopher Mellon

Budget Allocation Secrets

Mellon revealed that UAP programs receive funding through "black budget" allocations that bypass normal Congressional review. Some programs reportedly receive billions in classified appropriations with minimal oversight.

The National Security Risk

He emphasized that the lack of transparency creates vulnerabilities: "When our pilots don't know what they're dealing with, they can't properly defend against potential threats. This is a serious national security issue."
