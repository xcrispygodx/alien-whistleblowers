The Pentagon Witnesses Speak

In an unprecedented public session, three senior Pentagon officials testified about the UAP phenomenon before the Senate Armed Services Committee.

Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Statements

The head of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) confirmed that approximately 10% of UAP cases remain truly unidentified after rigorous analysis. When pressed about "exotic" capabilities, he acknowledged some objects demonstrate "acceleration rates we cannot explain with current technology."

"When you're talking about objects that can accelerate to significant fractions of light speed in less than a second... that's not something we can replicate today." — Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick, AARO Director

Admiral Gough's Testimony

The Deputy Chief of Naval Operations for Undersea Warfare addressed the threat assessment: "We have observed UAP exhibiting flight characteristics that could pose challenges to naval aviation safety and operational security."

The Congressional Response

Senators expressed concern over the lack of transparency and the continued use of special access programs that bypass Congressional oversight. The hearing revealed that some programs have been operating for "over five decades" without substantive reporting to Congress.
